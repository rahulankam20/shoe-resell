# Taste: Art Direction & Visual Refinement

## Eliminating the "AI-Generated UI Smell"
Common AI generator mistakes to actively remove:
- ❌ Bloated border radiuses on every single element (e.g. `rounded-3xl` everywhere).
- ❌ Multi-colored pastel gradient text and borders.
- ❌ Heavy, blurry, generic box-shadows on card grids.
- ❌ Centering every single text block regardless of context.
- ❌ Gigantic, meaningless hero taglines without editorial punch.
- ❌ Overpopulated badges, chips, and icons competing for attention.

## The High-Fashion Footwear Visual Standard (SSENSE / Grailed / Kith / NikeLab)
1. **Typography Hierarchy**:
   - High-contrast grotesque or clean geometric sans-serif headings with tight letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` to `-0.03em`).
   - Clean, mono or tabular numerics for prices (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
   - Uppercase micro-labels (`0.65rem` - `0.75rem`) with generous tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em` - `0.14em`).
2. **Restrained Color & Contrast**:
   - Monochromatic foundation (Deep onyx `#09090b`, Off-black `#111113`, Slate white `#f8f8fa`, Clean white `#ffffff`).
   - Muted border tones: subtle `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` on dark / `1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.07)` on light.
   - Restrained accent touches (e.g., volt/infrared or deep crimson) used strictly for sale badges or critical alerts.
3. **Intentional Product Cards**:
   - Neutral/clean backdrop containers for shoe photography.
   - Minimalist meta layout: Brand/Model in crisp weight, subtle price alignment, minimal framing.
   - Avoid nesting cards inside cards.
4. **Art Direction & Balance**:
   - Asymmetric grid accents and editorial photography framing.
   - Generous, deliberate negative space allowing footwear silhouettes to command visual focus.
